# Derja and Arabizi Mixing Strategy

This document defines the compositional breakdown for Derja and Arabizi content within the training data mixture. No specific datasets are referenced here; this is a strategic allocation plan only.

## Arabizi (35% total)

Of the 35% allocated to Arabizi content:

- **20%** — Regular Arabizi: everyday conversational and informal Tunisian text written in Latin script with numeric markers (e.g., 3, 7, 5, 9).
- **15%** — Math and coding using Arabizi: technical, mathematical, and programming content expressed in Arabizi script.

## Derja (25% total)

Of the 25% allocated to Derja content:

- **15%** — Regular Derja: everyday conversational and informal Tunisian text written in Arabic script.
- **10%** — Math and coding using Derja: technical, mathematical, and programming content expressed in Derja (Arabic script).

## Summary

| Script | Share of Total | Subcategory | Share of Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arabizi | 35% | Regular Arabizi | 20% |
| Arabizi | 35% | Math & Coding (Arabizi) | 15% |
| Derja | 25% | Regular Derja | 15% |
| Derja | 25% | Math & Coding (Derja) | 10% |
